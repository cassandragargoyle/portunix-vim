# Question: Customer Identity

## ID
q01-customer-identity

## Question
Who is the customer on this document?

## Expected Answer Type
- Name (required)
- Address (optional)
- Customer ID (optional)
- Email (optional)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Correct name identification
- Distinguishing customer from recipient/supplier/provider
- Not confusing with document issuer (company sending the invoice)

## Notes
On invoices, the customer is typically the person/company receiving and paying the invoice.
The customer may be labeled as "Zákazník", "Odběratel", "Customer", "Buyer".

# Question: Payment Amount

## ID
q02-payment-amount

## Question
What is the total amount to pay on this document?

## Expected Answer Type
- Amount (required)
- Currency (required)
- Due date (optional)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Correct amount identification
- Not confusing with subtotals, VAT amounts, or deposit amounts
- Identifying the final "to pay" amount

## Notes
Look for terms like "K úhradě", "Celkem", "Total", "Amount due", "Doplatek".
Be careful to distinguish from prepaid amounts or deposits.

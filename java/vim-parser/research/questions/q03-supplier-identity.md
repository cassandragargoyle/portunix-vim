# Question: Supplier Identity

## ID
q03-supplier-identity

## Question
Who is the supplier/issuer of this document?

## Expected Answer Type
- Company name (required)
- Company ID/IČO (optional)
- Address (optional)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Correct company identification
- Not confusing with customer or recipient
- Identifying the entity that issued/sent the document

## Notes
The supplier is typically shown in header/footer or has prominent branding.
Look for "Dodavatel", "Vystavil", "Supplier", "From", company logo area.
